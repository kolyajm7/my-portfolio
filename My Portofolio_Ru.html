02 · Founder

The Founder's Story

In 2017, with one digital tablet, two boxes of storyboards, and six like-minded people, Hwan Frame was founded in an old workspace.

Years later, that small space — where we even conserved air conditioning — has grown into a team of nearly dozens of accomplished animation professionals.

I still maintain one habit: I personally draw the first concept sketch for every project.

04 · Core

Our Core: “The Power of Drawing by Hand”

Hwan Frame's greatest asset is its nearly dozens of animation professionals. Key animators, in-between animators, animation directors, background artists, and compositing specialists each handle their own fields.

Our most distinct strength is our capability in traditional hand-drawn concept art. Hand-drawn concept art does not simply mean “drawing by hand.” It is a creative act of directly controlling a character’s breath, center, and the subtle tremors of their emotions frame by frame.

05 · Trend

AI Is Powerful, but Two Hands Cannot Be Replaced

In 2025, the AI penetration rate among surveyed animation companies in China reached 90.3%, and more than half officially adopted AI tools in production.

However, generative AI has made remarkable progress in visual realism but is difficult to replace human animators in the field of 'acting' — the core of character animation.

AI is a capable assistant at our workbench, but what ultimately determines whether a character is 'alive' is still the animator's hands, eyes, and judgment.

06 · Tools

The Tools in Our Hands

Clip Studio Paint EX RETAS Studio Moho Pro OpenToonz Toon Boom Harmony After Effects TVPaint Blender Grease Pencil Pro Motion Maya ZBrush Houdini Arnold / RenderMan Unreal Engine Nuke Silhouette Flow Production Tracking

-----

These are 2D and 3D animation production tools, movie VFX production tools, and 2D pixel art and dot production tools. You can directly download and install the necessary tools.

Clip StudioPaint and RETAS Studio are our team's primary production tools. While CSP excels in hand-drawn original artwork, intermediate drawings, and storyboarding, RETAS demonstrates strength in mass production and the division of labor that extends from line art and coloring to photography. We strategically combine and utilize these two core tools depending on the nature of the project.

Moho (also known as Anime Studio) is 2D animation software that we use as an auxiliary tool in specific projects.

07 · Education

Independent Education System: Keeping Skills Unbroken

In-house training program — step-by-step curricula from new animators to veterans, based on a mentor-apprentice system across key animation, in-between, background, and compositing.

Hand-drawn key animation succession project — veteran key animators personally teach young people in a permanently operated program.

External workshops, online learning content, and internship acceptance promote skill improvement across the entire team.

This educational system is not merely about teaching technology. It is also a venue for conveying to the next generation the philosophy of work of *Circular Frame*: 'Breathing a soul into every frame with human hands.' No matter how advanced AI becomes, education by humans is indispensable to cultivate its judgment and sensibility.
